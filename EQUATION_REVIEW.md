# Equation and convention review

Review date: 11 October 2026. This is an algebraic, dimensional and analytical-limit review of the supplied teaching material. It is not independent human peer review. Sources are linked in the chapters and [bibliography](references.md); implementation checks are in [the test suite](tests/test_numerics.py).

The review proceeds from the target probability measure or mechanical definition, then checks the formula separately from the implementation. [CONVENTIONS.md](docs/CONVENTIONS.md) controls units, pair counting, pressure signs and current definitions.

| Formula family | Independent reasoning and dimensional check | Implementation evidence or remaining scope |
|---|---|---|
| Canonical probability | Normalizing the Boltzmann weight gives an ensemble average; beta times energy is dimensionless. Classical partition measures include the momentum measure and indistinguishability factors where required. | Notebook 1 normalizes discrete states. Ergodicity remains a sampling assumption. |
| Newtonian dynamics | Force is the negative position gradient of potential energy: energy/length = mass × acceleration. Velocity Verlet follows symmetric half velocity, full position, half velocity updates. | Finite-difference force comparison, Newton's third law, reversibility and decreasing energy error with timestep. |
| LJ cutoff treatment | Subtracting the value and tangent at the cutoff makes both potential and radial force continuous. The derivative sign is checked against finite differences. | Python tests continuity and force consistency. The LAMMPS input uses a different, explicitly documented potential-shift convention. |
| Temperature | Equipartition applies to independent classical quadratic momentum degrees of freedom. Constraints and COM removal reduce the count. Unequal-mass COM velocity must be mass weighted. | LJ uses 3N−3. OpenMM counts system constraints and three removed COM degrees of freedom. Finite-timestep kinetic conventions remain engine dependent. |
| Virial pressure | The kinetic and pair virial terms each have energy units; division by 3V gives pressure in 3D. A repulsive pair contributes positive virial with the defined separation/force convention. | LJ force and virial use the same pair vectors. Long-range, constraint and many-body terms must come from their engine implementations. |
| Metropolis–Hastings | Detailed balance requires the target-density ratio multiplied by the reverse/forward proposal-density ratio. Rejected attempts retain the state in the sample series. | Harmonic MC and ideal-gas GCMC notebooks include rejected states. Ordinary MC has no physical time coordinate. |
| GCMC | The grand measure and uniform insertion/deletion proposals give activity × volume divided by N+1 for insertion, and its reverse N/(activity × volume) for deletion. Activity has inverse-volume units. Move-selection ratios are retained in the general formulas. | Equal-probability moves include empty deletion attempts. Ideal-gas number mean and variance approach the Poisson prediction. Molecular internal partition and configurational-bias factors need consistent definitions. |
| KMC | An exponential survival law with total rate produces a waiting time in seconds when rates are in inverse seconds. Event probabilities are proportional to rates. State occupation must be weighted by residence duration. | A two-state chain recovers its analytic stationary time fractions; jump-count fractions would be wrong. |
| RDF and coordination | Same-species unordered pairs require a factor of two and N(N−1); disjoint cross-species pairs require NaNb. The shell-volume ratio makes g dimensionless. Integrating with the matching reference number density returns neighbor counts. | Random ideal-gas tests check same and cross species independently. Shell radii stay within half the cubic cell. Pores require a different accessible-volume reference. |
| Einstein/VACF diffusion | Integrating velocity twice gives MSD as twice the convolution of time minus lag with the velocity autocorrelation. Its long-time slope yields the factor 1/(2d); the VACF integral has factor 1/d. | Ballistic trajectories, a known linear MSD and explicit dimensionality are tested. No converged material diffusion coefficient is inferred from short runs. |
| Green–Kubo viscosity | Intensive pressure correlation has units Pa²; multiplying its time integral by V/(kBT) gives Pa s. | Reviewed analytically. No viscosity calculation is claimed. Stress and pressure sign conventions must be consistent. |
| Green–Kubo heat transport | Extensive heat current has units energy × length/time. The prefactor is 1/(3VkBT²); using flux density instead introduces V/(3kBT²). The resulting units are W/(m K). | Reviewed analytically. No thermal-conductivity execution or universal many-body heat-current implementation is claimed. |
| Uncertainty | Summing stationary lag covariances gives statistical inefficiency 1+2 sum(rho), effective sample size n/g and mean variance approximately g times variance/n. Correlation time is g times saved interval/2 under the stated convention. | AR(1) provides an analytic correlation benchmark. Blocking records discarded tails; Student intervals assume sufficiently independent, approximately well-behaved block means. |
| Umbrella/WHAM | Removing the window bias relates biased and unbiased probabilities through a window normalization. The denominator uses exp(beta f minus beta w). Histogram bin probabilities sum to one; density divides by bin width. | Exact constructed histograms recover an analytical distribution; disconnected overlap is rejected. Toy MC adds sampling error. No automatic WHAM confidence interval is supplied. |
| Metadynamics/replica exchange | Well-tempered bias approaches minus (1−1/gamma) times the free energy under its asymptotic assumptions. Swapping canonical configurations uses the difference of inverse temperatures times the difference of energies. | Analytical sign review only. Biased crossing times are not interpreted as ordinary rates. |
| PIMD | The ring-polymer measure uses inverse temperature beta/P and spring frequency P/(beta hbar) together; changing one convention requires changing the Hamiltonian/thermostat consistently. | Notebook 6 checks analytical oscillator limits. No bead-trajectory or quantum real-time simulation is claimed. |
| CPMD | The auxiliary orbital kinetic term is a numerical extended-system term. The Lagrange multipliers enforce orthonormality; nuclear dynamics requires adiabatic separation. | Schematic equations are identified as such. No fictitious electronic timescale is called physical electron dynamics. |
| Resource arithmetic | Number of integration steps is duration/timestep with units aligned; raw coordinate bytes are frames × atoms × components × bytes/component. Output conventions determine whether the initial frame adds one. | Unit tests check integer steps and frame/storage arithmetic. Hardware rates and allocation ranges are explicitly illustrative. |

## Syntax and rendering scope

Display equations use GitHub math fences; inline expressions use dollar delimiters. The offline checker checks fences, braces and local navigation. The validation record separately reports parsing of extracted expressions with KaTeX. Successful parsing does not prove the physics, and this archive has not been published to test live GitHub rendering.

## Deliberate boundaries

Theory can be correct while a chosen model is inaccurate, a trajectory is too short, or an engine input fails in another release. The scientific review therefore does not convert static CP2K/RASPA3/LAMMPS checks into execution claims, nor short trajectories into numerical or statistical convergence. A researcher should apply the [checklist](CHECKLIST.md) to their own system.
